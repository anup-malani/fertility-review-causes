### Marriage-market quality, assortative mating, and fertility

- Claim cluster: Partner economic quality, education mismatch, and search frictions can alter union
  formation and fertility.
- Representative papers: Preliminary candidates are indexed in
  `literature/search-logs/marriage-market-economics-scoping-candidates.csv`.
- Methods/data: Gender-specific labor-demand shocks, schooling reforms, matching decompositions,
  census descriptions, and couple-level fertility comparisons.
- Agreement/disagreement: Economic shocks to men's prospects appear to move births, but the early
  evidence does not support marriage as the only mediator. Most assortative-mating studies do not
  identify a fertility effect.
- Relevance to current paper: Supplies the evidence map for
  `output/chapters/marriage-market-economics.md`.
- Missing evidence: Exogenous changes in match composition linked to completed fertility and a
  formal mediation decomposition.
