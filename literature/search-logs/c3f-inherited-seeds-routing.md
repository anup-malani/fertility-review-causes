## Three calls this triage raises

1. **Is the Value-of-Children survey tradition evidence or background?** 515 records carry the term,
   47% of the frame; 191 sit inside the fertility restriction. It measures a *stated valuation* of
   children, not a realized flow, and its outcome is usually an intention. Recommendation: background
   and mechanism evidence on link 1, never pooled and never a demsig numerator — the same treatment
   `outcome-level-separates-channels` gave C.3.e.
2. **Are migrant remittances to parents an upward flow for our purposes?** W4323315823 (Jamaica) and
   W3207094258 (Morocco) both estimate remittances → fertility. They are literally child-to-parent
   transfers. But D.1.b read the Morocco mechanism as income and human capital, which is C.1.a.
   Recommendation: in scope only where the estimate is signed on the *transfer* rather than on
   household income, and tagged `shared_with: C.1.a`.
3. **Where is the C.3.a wall?** Three of four primary seeds move the flow through child labour
   demand, which is C.3.a's mechanism, and C.3.a is unstarted (union frame 1811). Drawing this wall
   unilaterally is the boundary debt that disqualified A.6 and A.3 in the ranking, so it needs
   stating rather than assuming. Recommendation: C.3.a owns cross-*population* variation in baseline
   child productive value; C.3.f owns *change over time* in the net flow within a population.
