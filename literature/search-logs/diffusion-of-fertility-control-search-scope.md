## Causal claim

Fertility decline spreads as an *idea*. Once fertility control — the knowledge that births can be
deliberately limited, and the sense that doing so is acceptable — enters a population, it propagates
through social, linguistic, and religious contact, so that adoption at one place and time raises
adoption nearby, faster and along different lines than economic conditions alone would predict. The
distinguishing empirical signature is the Princeton European Fertility Project residual: marital
fertility transitions in 19th- and early-20th-century Europe clustered by language and religion and
diffused across provincial borders on a timetable that development indicators do not reproduce. The
mechanism is registered as **proximate** — it describes the *spread* of fertility control, not the
underlying value change that made small families desirable (that is D.1) and not the physical
technology that made control cheap (that is A.2).

## This chapter's parameter (the §4 chain)

A.3 is one link in a proximate chain and owns exactly one parameter: **the effect of social exposure
to fertility control on its adoption**, net of the economic and cultural drivers that operate through
other chapters. Concretely, the estimate this chapter seeks is the effect on an individual's or a
region's adoption of fertility control of *others having already adopted it* — peers, prior cohorts,
or a linguistically/religiously proximate community — or of the idea's legitimacy having arrived,
holding fixed:

- **economic conditions** (income, child cost, mode of production — category C);
- **the underlying value change** — why small families became desirable — which is **D.1.a**
  (postmaterialism/secularization) and **D.1.b** (Westernization). A.3 carries *what spreads* only as
  a diffusing object, not as the value whose change is to be explained;
- **the contraceptive technology** itself (Pill, IUD, sterilization), which is **A.2**;
- **exogenous program supply** of information and access, which is **A.5**.

What is spread → D.1. The channel it travels through → A.20. Whether it spreads at all, socially and
faster than economics predicts → **this chapter**.

## Boundary walls (the load-bearing routing rules)

A.3 sits inside a dense neighbourhood of proximate and cultural hypotheses and shares vocabulary and
seminals with several. Routing is the central screening task. The `405` diagnostics
(`a3-term-diagnostics-2026-09-17.md`) measured each wall's overlap; the overlaps are small (1–59
records) but the vocabularies touch, so the discriminators below are stated at the level of the
*identified parameter*, not the keyword.

**Wall 1 — A.3 vs A.20 (Cultural Diffusion Mechanisms, `cultural-diffusion-mechanisms`). LOAD-BEARING;
RESOLVED 2026-09-18 in favor of channel-excluded A.3 (see Status).** A.3 owns the *object* that diffuses — the information about and legitimation of
fertility control, and the claim that this object travels independent of economic conditions. A.20
owns the *channels* through which any cultural content moves — social networks, mass media (radio, TV,
soap operas), and linguistic/cultural community boundaries — "independent of the content of those
norms." A paper whose estimate is the effect of a *channel* (a TV signal, a network tie, a language
border) on the pace or geography of change routes to A.20; a paper whose estimate is that fertility
control *as an idea* spread and was adopted independent of economics stays here. This is a content
(A.3) vs. conduit (A.20) line. It is defensible from both registry entries but thin: both are
registered proximate, both share Coale and Watkins 1986 and Bongaarts and Watkins 1996, and A.20's
note says it *absorbs* `social-network-peer-effects`, `ideational-diffusion-mass-media`, and
`linguistic-cultural-boundaries-princeton`. Because the Princeton linguistic-boundary evidence is
A.3's own signature and also named inside A.20's absorb-list, the two hypotheses can lay claim to the
same classic studies. **This scope draws the wall by content-vs-channel and does not annex any channel
term; whether A.3 and A.20 should remain distinct hypotheses at all is escalated to the PI** (see the
park note and the Escalation entry in the ticket log). Channel terms are therefore excluded from A.3's
block, not because they are irrelevant to diffusion but because they are A.20's registered territory.

**Wall 2 — A.3 vs A.2 (Modern Contraceptive Technology and Diffusion, `contraceptive-technology-diffusion`).**
Both names contain "diffusion." A.2 is the diffusion of the physical *methods* — the Pill, the IUD,
modern sterilization — that lower the effort and uncertainty of avoiding a birth. A.3 is the diffusion
of the *idea and its legitimacy*, which can spread before, without, or independent of any new method
(the historical European transitions ran on withdrawal and abstinence, not the Pill). Discriminator: a
study identifying off the arrival or cost of a *technology* (Comstock repeal, state-level Pill access,
sterilization campaigns) is **A.2**; a study identifying off the spread of *knowledge or acceptability*
of control with the method held fixed is **A.3**.

**Wall 3 — A.3 vs A.6 (Reduction in Stigma Around Contraception and Abortion, `stigma-reduction-contraception-abortion`).**
A.3 and A.6 share the registered word *legitimation*: A.6's claim is that "cultural legitimation of
contraception and abortion ... lowers fertility by enabling latent demand for fertility control to be
realized." The distinction is stock vs. flow. A.6 is the *level* of acceptability — how much stigma
attaches to contraception at a place and time, and whether reducing it releases latent demand. A.3 is
the *spread* of that acceptability (and of information) across social and cultural space — the
diffusion dynamic, the clustering, the border-crossing timetable. Discriminator: does the paper
identify off *how acceptable* control is (A.6) or off *its propagation* from adopters to non-adopters
and across linguistic/religious lines (A.3)? Because "legitimation" is A.6's registered construct as
much as A.3's, it must never be counted to A.3 as a free-standing term (see the park resolution).

**Wall 4 — A.3 vs A.19 (Intergenerational Transmission of Fertility Preferences, `intergenerational-transmission-fertility`).**
A.19 is *vertical* transmission within the family — parents' fertility or preferences predicting their
children's. A.3 is *horizontal* social diffusion across peers, cohorts, and communities. A
parent-to-child preference-correlation study is A.19; a peer-effect or community-exposure study is A.3.

**Wall 5 — A.3 vs A.5 (Organized Family Planning Programs and Access, `family-planning-programs`).**
A.5 is *exogenous program supply* — a government or NGO campaign that pushes information and access
into a population. A.3 is *endogenous* social diffusion that would occur through ordinary contact
absent any program. A study identifying off a program rollout is A.5. Program-induced diffusion
spillovers (a campaign in one village raising adoption in an untreated neighbour through social
contact) are a **bridge cell** — the spillover itself is A.3's parameter even though the shock is
A.5's.

**Wall 6 — A.3 vs D.1.a / D.1.b (root cultural cause).** A.3 is proximate and explicitly "describes the
spread mechanism, not what is being spread or why people adopt it." The value change — the shift toward
autonomy, secular authority, and small-family norms — is **D.1.a** (postmaterialism/secularization) and
its Westernization variant **D.1.b**. A paper explaining *why the desired family size fell* routes to
D.1; a paper explaining *how the resulting practice spread* stays here. Overlap with D.1.a is 33
records and with D.1.b 1 record — the walls are nearly disjoint at the keyword level; the risk is
conceptual, not lexical.

## Resolving the 405 park

The ticket was parked because `405` reported A.3's corrected frame at **2,419**, above C.3.a's 1,808.
Two pricing choices produced that number, and both are corrected here:

1. **`legitimation` was priced as an unconjoined `OR`.** `own + legitimation` measured 1,584 (a +865
   marginal gain) because the term was unioned into the frame free-standing, which imports the entire
   political- and institutional-legitimacy literature — none of it about birth control. A.3's
   registered construct is "legitimation *of birth control*," a conjunction. Conjoined to a
   birth-control context (and, per Wall 3, to a spread/diffusion design rather than a stigma-level
   one), its marginal contribution is a small fraction of 865. The general-legitimacy mass is not A.3's
   frame and is not searched.
2. **A.20's channel terms were charged to A.3.** The "block widened by ALL channel terms → 3,354" row
   is, in the diagnostics author's own words, "priced for the PI call, not proposed as a widening."
   Channels are A.20's registered territory (Wall 1) and are excluded from A.3's block. Charging them
   to A.3 is the same category error `405` caught in `404` — ranking a candidate against a frame that
   is not its own.

With `legitimation` conjoined and channels excluded, A.3's frame is the recorded pass-4 union of
**719** (re-measured 719 vs. the logged 727 on 2026-09-17, so it is stable), plus the small conjoined
increments for its genuinely-owned constructs (`knowledge of contraception` +272, `awareness of
contraception` +78, `European Fertility Project` +52, `parity` +52; the rest are ≤29). That lands well
below 1,808. A.3 clears its own gate once its frame is priced as its own.

**The A.20 question is resolved separately (2026-09-18): keep A.3 and A.20 distinct, channel-excluded.**
The frame size and the hypothesis-distinction are independent questions; the frame is settled above,
and the distinction is settled in favor of the content/channel wall (see Status). Only a PI decision to
*merge* A.3 into A.20 would reopen it and fold this scope into A.20's.

## Eligibility rules

- Include an empirical study only when its estimate bears on **the social spread or social learning of
  fertility control → adoption or fertility**, holding economic conditions fixed or adjusted.
- A study identifying off a *technology* (A.2), a *channel* (A.20), a *stigma level* (A.6), *vertical
  family transmission* (A.19), a *program* (A.5), or the *underlying value shift* (D.1) routes to that
  hypothesis, even where it mentions diffusion in passing.
- The formal diffusion/ideational canon (Rosero-Bixby–Casterline, Cleland–Wilson, Bongaarts–Watkins)
  and the descriptive Princeton volumes seed the **theory** stream and do not count toward empirical
  recall unless they carry an identified own estimate.
- No pre-modern cell: the mechanism presupposes an innovation that spreads, so evidence is FDT (the
  European transition) and SDT (contemporary LMIC and post-transition diffusion). Do not carry PM
  evidence.
- On every included empirical paper, record whether identification comes from **social exposure**
  (peer/cohort/community adoption) or **spatial/temporal spread** (province-level residual), and record
  what economic and value confounders the design holds fixed — the identification value of a diffusion
  estimate is set entirely by whether it separates social contagion from a common economic or cultural
  shock hitting neighbours at once (Manski's reflection problem is the standing threat).

## Expected shape of the evidence (a caution, not a result)

- **The identification threat is the reflection/common-shock problem, not routing.** A raw correlation
  in which neighbours adopt control together is equally consistent with genuine social diffusion and
  with a common economic or cultural shock arriving at once. Expect most historical Princeton-tradition
  evidence to be descriptive residual (development does not explain the clustering) rather than a
  design that identifies contagion; expect the credible identified estimates to be the modern micro
  peer-effect and program-spillover studies. Report this asymmetry rather than reading the descriptive
  residual as an identified diffusion effect.
- **The A.20 wall may move much of the strongest micro-causal evidence.** The Brazil/India TV studies
  (La Ferrara–Chong–Duryea, Jensen–Oster) are the cleanest identified estimates in this neighbourhood
  and, under Wall 1, they are *channel* estimates and route to A.20. A.3's identified core may be thin
  once channels are removed — which is itself the finding that motivates the PI call.
- **`405` in-frame markers, for calibration only** (not an evidence base): 16 of 719 records carry an
  identified-design marker (2.2%), 154 carry realized-fertility vocabulary, adoption-timing level is 0.
  A thin identified core is expected and is a result to report, not a search failure.

## Cold-start channels and leakage wall

1. Direct empirical papers estimating social exposure / spatial diffusion of fertility control →
   fertility, identified independently of the hypothesis list, seed the empirical Tier-A candidate set.
2. The formal diffusion/ideational theory canon and the Princeton descriptive volumes seed the theory
   set and do not count toward empirical recall.
3. References and citations of the independent seeds create the orthogonal Tier-B candidate frame.
4. Production-query terms are not mined from a paper and then evaluated on that same paper; learned
   extensions are fold-local after the gold frame exists.
