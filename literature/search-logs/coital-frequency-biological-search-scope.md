# Search scope — coital frequency and fecundability

**Hypothesis:** A.14 (HYPOTHESES-v5.md §A.14)
**Hypothesis slug:** `coital-frequency-biological`
**Target phenomena:** PM, FDT, SDT (all three, per registry). ## Causal claim

Not every act of unprotected intercourse can produce a birth, but no birth occurs without intercourse
(outside ART, which is A.17). The monthly probability that a fecund, non-contracepting couple
conceives — their **fecundability** — rises with **coital frequency**: more acts per cycle raise the
chance that one falls in the fertile window. Anything that lowers the frequency of intercourse
therefore lowers monthly fecundability and, cumulated over a reproductive life, realized fertility —
*mechanically, and independently of contraceptive use*. Coital frequency is one of the classic
Bongaarts–Davis–Blake proximate determinants: the final biological gate through which every distal
cause (economic, cultural, biological) must pass to change births.

The drivers of coital-frequency variation are many and span all three phenomena:

- **Spousal separation** — labour migration, seasonal work, military mobilisation and war, incarceration, commuter/"astronaut" and living-apart-together marriages — mechanically removes the opportunity for intercourse for the separated interval;
- **Customary and religious abstinence** — post-partum abstinence taboos, Ramadan and Lenten fasting, ritual periods — lower frequency in identifiable windows;
- **Health, age, and libido** — illness, declining sexual desire with age or ill-health, and (as registered drivers elsewhere) pharmacological suppression of libido;
- **The modern "sex recession"** — documented declines in sexual frequency among young and partnered adults in Japan, Korea, the US and the UK, invoked as a candidate SDT driver.

This chapter's mechanism is **biological/behavioural** (an exposure identity), not economic or
cultural: it holds fixed *why* frequency changes and estimates the effect of the frequency change
itself on conception and births. The distal causes are treated in their own chapters (see walls).

## This chapter's parameter (the §4 chain)

A.14 owns exactly one parameter: **the effect of coital frequency on monthly fecundability and
realized fertility — the frequency → fecundability → births identity — holding contraception,
sterility, and union status fixed.** Concretely, the estimate this chapter seeks is the change in the
per-cycle conception probability (or time-to-pregnancy, or the birth rate) produced by an
*exogenous change in coital frequency*, whatever its upstream cause. Two evidence streams identify it:

1. **The biometric relationship** — clinical/prospective studies relating measured coital frequency (or timing of intercourse relative to ovulation) to per-cycle conception probability or time-to-pregnancy (Barrett & Marshall 1969; Wilcox et al.; MacLeod & Gold). This estimates the *slope* of the identity.
2. **Exogenous frequency shocks → fertility** — natural experiments that move coital frequency for reasons plausibly unrelated to fertility desire: spousal separation from war mobilisation, labour or seasonal migration, incarceration; and abstinence windows (Ramadan, post-partum taboos). These identify the *demographic contribution* of frequency variation to births.

The chapter must hold fixed, and route elsewhere:

- **contraception and induced abortion** — the deliberate decoupling of intercourse from conception is the Bongaarts Cc/Ca indices — that is **A.2 / A.4 / A.5 / A.6**; A.14 is the exposure identity *net of* contraception;
- **union formation and the proportion married/partnered** — the Bongaarts Cm index (whether people are in a coital union at all) is **A.7 (marriage timing) / C.7 (marriage market) / A.23–A.24 (household and match formation)**; A.14 owns coital frequency *conditional on a union*, not the existence of the union;
- **post-partum infecundability / lactational amenorrhoea** — the Bongaarts Ci index — is **A.13**;
- **permanent sterility and age-related fecundity decline** — the couple's *capacity* to conceive at any frequency — is **A.15 (maternal age) / A.16 (paternal age) / B.3 (infectious sterility)**;
- **spontaneous intrauterine mortality** — conceptions lost before a live birth — is **B.5**;
- **the specific upstream causes of a frequency change** — digital leisure is **C.2.h**, antidepressant-induced low libido is **B.7**, the value shift away from sex/partnership is **D.1.a**.

Coital frequency → fecundability → births is this chapter. Why frequency changed (device, drug,
value, wage) → the relevant cause chapter. Whether a union exists at all → the marriage chapters.
Whether a conception survives → B.5. Whether the couple is fecund at all → A.15/A.16/B.3.

The completeness test in the probe prices each of these as `(baseline OR term) − baseline`. A large
gain from a genuine A.14 construct means the baseline was under-built and the term joins the frame
honestly; a large gain from a generic word conjoined to nothing — `sexual activity`, `libido`,
`separation` free-standing — is the free-`OR` inflater the A.3 park was built on, and is reported as
such, never folded in. No registered construct is priced as a free-standing `OR` that would import an
unrelated literature. In particular `separation` (statistics, chemistry, law), `activity` (physics,
chemistry), and `desire` are homonym-heavy and enter **only** conjoined with a coital/sexual topic
term and a fertility/fecundability outcome (see `HOMONYM`).

## Boundary walls (the load-bearing routing rules)

A.14 is the exposure gate in the Bongaarts framework, so it shares an edge with almost every other
proximate determinant. Routing is the central screening task; discriminators are stated at the level
of the *identified parameter*, not the keyword.

**Wall 1 — A.14 vs A.2/A.4/A.5/A.6 (Contraception and Abortion, the Cc/Ca indices). LOAD-BEARING.**
The claim is explicit that A.14 operates "independently of contraceptive use." Both change births per
act of intercourse, but the line is *what moves*. Contraception/abortion change the probability that
intercourse (or a conception) yields a birth, holding frequency fixed; A.14 changes the number of
acts, holding contraceptive practice fixed. Discriminator: a study identifying off contraceptive
adoption, method mix, or abortion access routes to **A.2–A.6**; a study identifying off a change in
coital frequency among couples with fixed contraceptive practice (or non-contracepting/natural-
fertility populations) stays here. A frequency study in a natural-fertility population is the cleanest
A.14 evidence precisely because Cc is held at its baseline.

**Wall 2 — A.14 vs A.7/C.7/A.23/A.24 (Marriage, Marriage Market, Household and Match Formation, the Cm index).**
Cm is whether a person is in a coital union at all; A.14 is how often intercourse occurs *given* a
union. Discriminator: a study whose variation is in the proportion married/partnered, age at
marriage, or match formation routes to the marriage chapters; a study whose variation is in coital
frequency *within* existing unions stays here. **Bridge cell:** spousal-separation studies (migration,
war) reduce coital frequency *within* an intact marriage — that is A.14, not A.7, because the union
persists and only exposure falls. A study that cannot separate "fewer unions" from "less sex within
unions" has not identified this parameter.

**Wall 3 — A.14 vs A.13 (Breastfeeding / Lactational Amenorrhoea, the Ci index).**
A.13 suppresses ovulation post-partum (no fecund cycle to expose); A.14 changes exposure within
fecund cycles. Both can be described as "post-partum" effects. Discriminator: a study whose mechanism
is anovulation/amenorrhoea from lactation routes to **A.13**; a study whose mechanism is reduced
intercourse (post-partum *abstinence* taboo, not lactational anovulation) stays here. The post-partum
taboo literature is A.14; the biology of lactational amenorrhoea is A.13, even when they co-occur.

**Wall 4 — A.14 vs A.15/A.16/B.3 (Fecundity Capacity: maternal age, paternal age, infectious sterility).**
These change the couple's *capacity* to conceive at any frequency (the fecundability ceiling); A.14
changes the frequency at a fixed capacity. Discriminator: a study identifying off age-related
fecundity decline, sperm quality, or sterilising infection routes to **A.15/A.16/B.3**; a study
identifying off frequency at fixed fecund capacity stays here. Age is a confounder A.14 studies must
address (older couples have both lower fecundity and lower frequency); a design that cannot separate
the two has not identified this parameter.

**Wall 5 — A.14 vs its upstream causes (C.2.h digital leisure, B.7 antidepressants, D.1.a values).**
A.14 owns the frequency → births identity *regardless of what changed frequency*; the specific cause
of the frequency change is owned by the cause chapter. Discriminator: a study whose estimand is
`cause → coital frequency` (digital leisure, a drug, a value shift → less sex) routes to the cause
chapter, with A.14 cited for the frequency→births link; a study whose estimand is
`coital frequency → fertility` stays here, reading the upstream cause only as the source of exogenous
variation. The "sex recession" descriptive literature that documents *falling frequency* without a
fertility outcome is mechanism/context (JEL §2), not empirical recall for this chapter unless it
carries the frequency→fertility link.

**Wall 6 — A.14 vs B.5 (Fetal Loss / Intrauterine Mortality).**
A.14 governs whether a conception occurs; B.5 governs whether a conception survives to a live birth.
Discriminator: a study whose outcome is the per-cycle *conception* probability, time-to-pregnancy, or
births-per-exposure routes here; a study whose outcome is the *loss* of an established pregnancy routes
to **B.5**.

No wall is declared unenforceable in advance. The A.7/C.7 union-vs-frequency bridge (Wall 2) and the
A.15/A.16 age confound (Wall 4) are the two most likely to need a full-text call.

## Eligibility rules

- **Include** if the study identifies the effect of coital frequency (or an exogenous shock to it) on
  conception probability, fecundability, time-to-pregnancy, or realized fertility, in humans, with
  contraceptive practice and union status either held fixed, controlled, or absent (natural fertility).
- **Exclude** if the estimand is a distal cause routed by a wall (contraception, union formation,
  lactation, fecundity capacity, fetal loss, or a specific upstream cause of frequency change) with no
  A.14 frequency→births link of its own; if the outcome is non-fertility; if it is a homonym; if it is
  ART-mediated conception (A.17); or if it is non-human without a stated human-demographic mapping.
- **Natural-fertility and historical-demography evidence is in scope for PM/FDT** (Hutterites, historical
  parish reconstitutions, post-partum-abstinence societies); modern clinical fecundability and the
  "sex recession" evidence is the SDT stream.

## Identification cautions

- **Reverse causality.** Wanting (or not wanting) a child changes coital frequency and its timing;
  the cleanest designs use frequency shocks plausibly unrelated to fertility desire (separation,
  ritual abstinence) or measure frequency prospectively before conception.
- **Age confound (Wall 4).** Older couples have both lower fecundity and lower frequency; the effect
  of frequency must be separated from the effect of ageing capacity.
- **The Bongaarts decomposition risk.** Because coital frequency sits inside the residual fecundability
  term rather than being one of the four named indices (Cm, Cc, Ca, Ci), some aggregate decompositions
  fold it into "natural fecundability" and cannot isolate it; such studies are mechanism/context, not
  a clean A.14 estimate.
- **Measurement.** Self-reported coital frequency is subject to recall and social-desirability bias;
  the biometric stream (prospective diaries) is higher-quality than cross-sectional recall.
