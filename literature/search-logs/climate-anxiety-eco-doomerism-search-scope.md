# Search scope — climate anxiety and eco-doomerism

**Hypothesis:** D.3.b (HYPOTHESES-v5.md)
**Hypothesis slug:** `climate-anxiety-eco-doomerism`
**Target phenomenon:** Second Demographic Transition (SDT) only; the sharp case is the post-2015 / 2020s cohort
**Status:** boundary walls FROZEN (Shravan, 2026-07-23) — hard line on all three walls (Wall 1
requires both ecological-fear content and a non-relabeled desire); stated-intention outcomes retained
in the primary synthesis, flagged as stated. Gold anchors not yet sourced. Ready for A2.

## Causal claim

A growing share of reproductive-age adults intentionally forgo or limit childbearing because of a
forward-looking *fear*: that the world will be uninhabitable or dangerous for their children, that
adding a person is ethically wrong given carbon emissions, or a generalized ecological pessimism about
the future. The distinguishing feature is that these individuals are **not indifferent to children** —
many strongly desire them — but **suppress a live desire out of dread.** This is a psychologically
mediated, fear-driven channel, distinct from a positive postmaterialist *preference* for a child-free
life (D.1.a) and from generalized clinical anxiety (D.3.a). Fertility falls **without** a fall in the
underlying preference for children.

## The three boundary walls (the load-bearing routing rules)

D.3.b is a *root* psychological cause whose empirical signature is easy to confuse with three
well-populated neighbors. Most candidate papers will belong to a neighbor, not here. Routing is
therefore the central screening task, exactly as the B.1/A.2 wall was for the sex-drive chapter.

**Wall 1 — D.3.b vs D.1.a (Postmaterialism, `postmaterialism-individualism-secularization`).** The
sharpest and most consequential distinction.
- **D.1.a asks:** *given a positive preference for self-actualization, autonomy, and non-child
  consumption, why do people choose fewer children?* Its mechanism is a value shift in which children
  compete with adult goals and lose. The desire for children has genuinely fallen.
- **D.3.b asks:** *why do people who may still want children forgo them?* Its mechanism is fear/dread
  about the ecological future, which suppresses a desire that remains positive.
- **Discriminator:** a paper is D.3.b-primary only if the childlessness/limitation is attributed to
  *fear about the ecological or planetary future* (habitability, emissions ethics, eco-doom), **and**
  the design does not simply relabel a positive freedom/career/self-fulfillment preference. A paper
  attributing low fertility to preferring freedom, career, travel, or voluntary child-freedom with no
  fear content is **D.1.a** and routes there.

**Wall 2 — D.3.b vs D.3.a (Mental Health / Anxiety Epidemic, `mental-health-anxiety-epidemic`).**
- **D.3.a asks:** *does the rising prevalence of clinical/subclinical anxiety and depression (a general
  disorder) impair pair-bonding, libido, and reproductive intention?* Content-agnostic affective/clinical
  impairment.
- **D.3.b asks:** *does a specific, content-laden fear about the climate/ecological future reduce
  fertility?*
- **Discriminator:** the feared object must be *ecological/planetary* specifically. Climate anxiety
  overlaps clinically with generalized anxiety disorder, but the *content* is what routes a paper here.
  A study of general anxiety/depression prevalence → fertility is **D.3.a**; a study of
  *climate-specific* worry → reproductive intention is **D.3.b**.

**Wall 3 — D.3.b vs C.5.a (Economic Uncertainty, `economic-uncertainty-and-unemployment`).**
- **C.5.a asks:** *does calculable personal income/employment risk induce rational option-value
  postponement?*
- **D.3.b asks the "future looks bad" question with different content and a different mechanism:* the
  feared object is planetary habitability or emissions ethics, not personal job/income security, and the
  mechanism is affect/dread rather than an option-value calculation on a return to normalcy.
- **Discriminator:** is the feared object the *habitability of the world / the ethics of adding
  emissions*, or the respondent's *own job/income/economic security*? The latter is **C.5.a**.

*(Lighter cross-ref — D.3.c Despair, `despair-hopelessness-fertility`: community-level collapse of
forward orientation driven by economic decline. D.3.b is distinguished by its specific ecological
content and its skew toward younger, higher-education, left-leaning cohorts. Note: C.5.a's cross-ref
field in HYPOTHESES-v5.md still labels despair as "D.3.b" — a stale tag from the v5 reorganization;
despair is D.3.c. Flagged for correction, does not affect this scope.)*

## Estimand cells

| Cell | Treatment / variation | Fertility outcome | Routing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PRIMARY_HABITABILITY_FEAR` | Fear that the world will be uninhabitable/dangerous for one's children | Reduced fertility intention or realized fertility, desire for children not reduced | Primary synthesis |
| `PRIMARY_CARBON_ETHICS` | Ethical concern that an additional child adds carbon emissions / anti-natalism-for-the-planet | Reduced fertility intention or behavior | Primary synthesis |
| `PRIMARY_ECO_PESSIMISM` | Generalized ecological pessimism / eco-doom about the collective future | Reduced fertility intention or behavior | Primary synthesis |
| `DESIRE_INDEPENDENCE` | Any of the above | Fertility/intention falls while the *desire* for children is positive or held fixed (fear suppresses a live desire) | Primary / bridge — the value-added cell |
| `ECO_ETHICS_THEORY` | Anti-natalist / eco-ethics philosophy and normative argument about procreation and the planet. Must carry **ecological** content | No empirical fertility estimate | Theory stream |
| `ANXIETY_CONSTRUCT` | Climate anxiety / eco-distress as the **object** of study — construct development, scale validation, prevalence, latent profiles, predictors | No fertility outcome (climate anxiety is the dependent variable) | Theory stream |
| `OFF_POSTMATERIALIST_D1a` | Positive self-actualization / autonomy preference, or secular value shift, with no fear content | Fertility | Route to D.1.a |
| `OFF_CLINICAL_D3a` | General (non-climate-specific) anxiety/depression | Reproductive intention / fertility | Route to D.3.a |
| `OFF_ECON_C5a` | Personal economic / job / income insecurity as the feared object | Fertility | Route to C.5.a |
| `OFF_OTHER` | Non-D.3.b determinant with no sibling-hypothesis home (conflict/war insecurity, partisanship, religious law, gender/care institutions, …) | Fertility | Route out; no sibling queue |
| `OFF_OUTCOME` | Ecological fear / eco-concern as a determinant of some **other** non-fertility outcome (migration, consumption, politics, mental health) | No fertility outcome | Mechanism / context only |
| `REVERSE` | Parenthood or fertility **status** affecting climate concern / eco-worry | Climate-attitude outcome | Context, not the effect sought here |
| `INSUFFICIENT_INFO` | Cannot be routed on the visible record (missing/uninformative abstract) | Unknown | Pairs only with `UNCERTAIN` |

**Taxonomy amendment (Shravan, 2026-07-24), after the A5 wave-1 screen audit.** Wave 1 (200 records,
5 blinded screeners) exposed four cell-level defects; see `{slug}-screen-wave1-audit.md`. Three changes
were approved: (G) `INSUFFICIENT_INFO` added, so abstract-less records stop being assigned cells they
have not earned — under v1 they inflated `THEORY`; (H) `OFF_OTHER` added, so the `OFF_*` routing labels
stop shipping junk into the D.1.a / D.3.a / C.5.a queues; (J) `THEORY` split into `ECO_ETHICS_THEORY`
and `ANXIETY_CONSTRUCT`, which also resolves the previously undecidable
climate-anxiety-as-dependent-variable class. `ECO_ETHICS_THEORY` is narrowed to *ecological*
anti-natalism — non-ecological (Benatar/Kantian harm-of-existence) anti-natalism routes to `OFF_OTHER`,
per Wall 2's own logic that the feared object must be specifically ecological.

**Considered and declined (Shravan, 2026-07-24):** widening `REVERSE` to cover parenthood *experience*
as well as *status*. `REVERSE` stays narrow; reverse-direction papers running through lived experience
or generativity route to `OFF_OUTCOME`. Both theory cells carry verdict `RELEVANT` and are separated
downstream — they do **not** count toward empirical recall.

## The realized-vs-intended level tag (not a routing cell, but mandatory)

Every included empirical paper is additionally tagged on the **outcome level**:
`STATED_INTENTION_OR_ATTITUDE` (desire, planned parity, reproductive intention, "climate is a reason
I will have fewer children") vs `REALIZED_FERTILITY` (completed or observed births / parity). This is
not a routing decision — both are in scope — but the distinction is load-bearing for synthesis, because
the literature is expected to be almost entirely intention/attitude and very thin on realized behavior
(see "Expected shape of the evidence").

**Synthesis rule (Shravan, 2026-07-23):** stated-intention / attitude outcomes are retained as a
first-class part of the primary synthesis — they are not relegated to context — but every estimate,
figure, and pooled number built on them carries the standing caveat that it measures *stated intention*,
not realized fertility. A realized-fertility pool is reported separately when the evidence exists; the
two levels are never combined into one pooled estimate.

## Eligibility rules

- Include empirical studies only when the estimate bears on **ecological/climate fear or eco-ethical
  concern → fertility intention or behavior** — not on climate attitudes with no reproductive outcome,
  and not on a positive child-free preference relabeled as concern.
- The anti-natalist / eco-ethics philosophical canon and pure climate-anxiety-construct psychometrics
  (scale-validation papers with no fertility outcome) seed the **theory** stream and do **not** count
  toward empirical recall.
- Phenomenon is **SDT only.** There is no pre-modern or FDT cell — the mechanism is a 21st-century
  phenomenon. Do not carry PM/FDT evidence.
- Stated-intention outcomes are in scope and are expected to dominate, but must be tagged
  `STATED_INTENTION_OR_ATTITUDE` and never pooled with realized fertility.
- A study identifying the effect purely off a positive postmaterialist/self-actualization preference,
  general clinical anxiety, or personal economic insecurity routes to D.1.a / D.3.a / C.5.a
  respectively, even if it mentions climate in passing.
- Political identity, education, and religiosity are **recorded on every included empirical paper** as
  candidate confounders (they predict both climate concern and low fertility) — see the identification
  caution below.
- Keep theory and off-cell papers discoverable but outside the empirical primary-cell recall denominator.

## When to adjudicate mechanisms

The title/abstract screen decides only whether a paper belongs in the empirical climate-fear stream,
the theory stream, or an adjacent/off-cell stream, and it does **not** require the RA to determine the
exact fear-content variety from an abstract. Detailed mechanism coding occurs during full-text
extraction, before synthesis.

For every included empirical paper, full-text extraction must distinguish:

- `HABITABILITY_FEAR` — the stated reason is that the world will be unsafe/uninhabitable for the child;
- `CARBON_ETHICS` — the stated reason is the emissions / carbon footprint of an additional person;
- `ECO_PESSIMISM_GENERAL` — a diffuse ecological doom with no single specified content;
- `DESIRE_HELD_POSITIVE` — the design establishes that respondents *want* children yet forgo/limit them
  because of the fear (the clause that separates D.3.b from D.1.a);
- `MIXED_OR_UNCLEAR` — climate cited among several reasons with no isolated channel.

Also record the **outcome level** (stated intention/attitude vs realized fertility) and the **candidate
confounder set actually adjusted for** (political identity, education, income, religiosity,
general/clinical anxiety), because the identification value of the estimate is set by which of these the
design holds fixed.

Drafting may report only mechanisms supported by these full-text fields. A cross-sectional correlation
of climate worry with low fertility *intention* that adjusts for none of the confounders may document
the association but must not be described as evidence that fear *causes* the fertility reduction, nor
that the desire for children was held fixed.

## Expected shape of the evidence (a caution, not a result)

D.3.b is an emerging phenomenon primarily documented in the 2020s. Its canonical sources (YouGov
2021–2024 surveys, Schneider-Mayerson and Leong 2020, Bastianelli et al. 2025, Britt et al. 2025)
document **belief and stated intention**, not realized fertility with credible causal identification.
Three consequences to expect and to report honestly:

1. **Cold-start channel 1 (prior meta-analyses / systematic-review included-study lists) is likely thin
   but not necessarily empty** — the 2025 *Genus* and *J. Marriage & Family* entries may themselves be
   scoping/systematic reviews of the climate-concern → fertility-intention literature. A near-empty or
   review-only channel 1 is itself a finding, not a search failure.
2. **The estimand-ready pooling set for *realized* fertility may be near-empty**, while the
   stated-intention / attitude stream and the qualitative stream are comparatively rich. The review
   should surface this intention-vs-behavior asymmetry rather than manufacture a realized-fertility
   pooled estimate the literature does not support.
3. **The central identification threat is the D.1.a confound, not merely a routing nuisance.**
   Left-leaning political identity, higher education, secularism, and postmaterialist values jointly
   predict both elevated climate concern and low fertility. A raw correlation of climate worry with low
   fertility intention could be entirely a postmaterialist value shift (D.1.a) with climate concern as a
   correlate rather than a cause. The `DESIRE_HELD_POSITIVE` cell and the recorded confounder set exist
   precisely to separate the fear channel from the preference channel; expect few designs to achieve it.

## Cold-start channels and leakage wall

1. Direct empirical papers estimating climate/ecological fear or eco-ethical concern → fertility
   intention or behavior, identified independently of the hypothesis list, seed the empirical Tier-A
   candidate set.
2. Anti-natalist / eco-ethics philosophy and climate-anxiety psychometric-construct papers seed the
   theory set but do not count toward empirical recall.
3. References and citations of the independent seeds create the orthogonal Tier-B candidate frame.
4. Production-query terms will not be mined from a paper and then evaluated on that same paper; learned
   extensions must be fold-local after the gold frame exists.

## Pre-query anchor audit (not yet built)

The verified candidate anchor set will be stored in
`climate-anxiety-eco-doomerism-cold-start-anchors.json`. Every anchor must clear the **mandatory
existence-verification gate** (a live DOI, or a Crossref/publisher record confirming the title exists)
before it enters any recall denominator — no anchor is hand-asserted from memory. Because this is a
recent, fast-moving literature with heavy preprint and survey-report (grey-literature) representation,
the existence gate is especially load-bearing: report-style sources (YouGov, Pew, Morning Consult) that
lack a DOI are kept only when a stable publisher URL confirms them, and are flagged as grey literature.
The set will deliberately contain habitability-fear, carbon-ethics, eco-pessimism, theory, and
off-cell (D.1.a / D.3.a / C.5.a) decoy anchors so the eventual search is tested on routing as well as
topical retrieval.
