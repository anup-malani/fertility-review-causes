## What retrieval cannot fix

The gap in this chapter is not a retrieval gap. Job A holds twenty records and the screen found no study anywhere in a 6,798-record frame that estimates antidepressant exposure against a population fertility quantity with a design that separates the medication from the indication. Retrieving all twenty in full does not change that, and the chapter should not read as though a successful fetch would. What retrieval decides here is whether the twenty can be *characterised* precisely enough to say what each one does and does not identify.
