# Stage 10 demographic significance — art-access-fertility-recovery (A.17)

> **PROVISIONAL: computed on 33 of 131 wanted full texts.** Every share below is an UPPER BOUND, because 9 of the 14 arm-1 records in hand compute ART's contribution without confronting the counterfactual — they treat every ART birth as one that would not otherwise have occurred.

## PM and FDT

**NOT ASSESSED, and not for lack of evidence.** The first IVF birth was in 1978 and ART is demographically negligible before the mid-1990s. Pre-modern and First-Demographic-Transition fertility cannot be affected by a technology that does not exist. If it had existed the sign would be positive; the registry does not enumerate these arms and this chapter does not invent them.

## S4 — the units check, before any arithmetic

**The phenomenon to be explained is measured in whole children per woman; this mechanism offers a share of births running between about 1% and 7%.**

On A.12 a units check of this shape ended the chapter: a multiplier bounded between 1.01 and 1.05 cannot explain a phenomenon denominated in whole children, and no study could have changed that. **It does not end this one.** A few percent of a TFR is not nothing when the decline to be explained is around one child, so A.17 has to do the arithmetic A.12 could skip. That is the difference between a mechanism that is the wrong size and a mechanism that is merely small.

### Check against the one paper that reports the pair itself

Czechia is the only case where a retrieved paper states the with-and-without TFR directly: **1.71 with ART against 1.65 without**, a contribution of 0.06 children. Against the 1965 baseline of 2.18 that is an offset of **11.3%**, against this script's reconstruction of 11.8%. The two agree closely, which is the point of running both.

## A denominator warning the arithmetic above cannot fix

Three of the five shares are shares **of births**; two are shares **of the TFR**. They are not the same quantity. Converting a birth share into a TFR contribution assumes the ART share is constant across the age schedule, and it is not — **ART is concentrated at older ages**, where age-specific fertility rates are low. The Italian data make this visible: MAR is 3.7% of the TFR overall and **16% among the oldest mothers**. Applying a flat birth share therefore biases the implied contribution UPWARD, in the same direction as the counterfactual error. Both errors inflate; neither offsets the other.

## The endogeneity check, which is the largest correction and is unquantified

**ART demand is generated by postponement.** The mechanism's own movement is caused, in substantial part, by the phenomenon it is claimed to offset: women seek treatment because they began later. A.12 met the same structure — a quarter of the modern twinning rise is driven by delayed childbearing — and had to net it out before claiming any offset. **The feedback is larger here, not smaller**, because A.12's channel was compositional while this one is the whole demand mechanism.

A component of the offset that exists only because the decline occurred is not evidence against the decline's cause. **No study in hand decomposes the share into an induced-by-postponement part and an independent part**, so the correction's magnitude is unknown and its direction is not: it reduces every figure in the table above.

## S5 — the verdict sentence

**For the Second Demographic Transition, the verdict is MINOR, because ART's contribution to the TFR runs between 2.7% and 11.8% of the observed decline across the five settings measured, and every one of those figures is an upper bound before the counterfactual and postponement-feedback corrections are applied.**

**For pre-modern variation, the verdict is NOT ASSESSED, because the technology did not exist.**
